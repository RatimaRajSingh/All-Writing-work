## AI Course Creation Prompt

## Project Overview

This document demonstrates how I use AI responsibly and intentionally at the course conception stage to accelerate instructional design while maintaining human-led decision-making, quality, and alignment with learning outcomes.

The prompt below was used in an AI-assisted course creation tool to generate a short, scenario-based corporate learning module that can be built in Articulate Storyline.

---

## Course Context

**Course Title:** Using Generative AI Responsibly at Work  
**Audience:** Corporate professionals / knowledge workers  
**Duration:** 10–12 minutes  
**Level:** Beginner → Applied  
**Delivery Format:** Interactive eLearning (Articulate Storyline)

---

## AI Prompt Used for Course Generation

Create a short, scenario-based corporate learning module titled **“Using Generative AI Responsibly at Work.”**

### Audience
Corporate professionals and knowledge workers with little to no prior AI background.

### Duration
10–12 minutes

### Level
Beginner to applied

---

### Learning Goals

- Help learners understand what generative AI can and cannot do in a workplace context  
- Teach basic rules for responsible and confidential use of AI tools  
- Enable learners to make safe, ethical decisions when using AI at work  

---

### Instructional Approach

- Start with a real-world workplace scenario to hook learners  
- Use short, clear explanations with minimal theory  
- Include interactive decision-making scenarios with feedback  
- Add light gamification (choices, consequences, retry) tied directly to learning outcomes  
- End with a short knowledge check and key takeaways  

---

### Tone & Style

- Professional, simple, and practical  
- Corporate-friendly language  
- Focus on real workplace decisions rather than technical AI details  

---

### Constraints

- Do not use confidential or company-specific data  
- Emphasize human accountability and review of AI-generated output  

---

### Output Requirement

Generate a clear course outline with lessons, interactions, and assessment ideas suitable for building in Articulate Storyline.

---

## Why This Prompt Was Designed This Way

- Clearly defines audience, duration, and level to avoid overgeneration  
- Anchors the course in scenario-based learning and applied decision-making  
- Explicitly ties gamification to learning outcomes, not engagement alone  
- Includes ethical and confidentiality constraints to model responsible AI use  
- Ensures outputs are instructionally sound and tool-ready, not generic content  

---

## Role of AI vs Human Judgment

### AI Was Used To:

- Accelerate initial course framing and outlining  
- Generate a structured starting point for lessons and interactions  

### Human Instructional Design Decisions Included:

- Final learning objective wording and Bloom’s alignment  
- Scenario realism and feedback quality  
- Assessment validity and instructional flow  
- Suitability for Articulate Storyline interactions  

---

## Design Rationale Summary

AI was intentionally used only at the ideation and outline stage to improve speed and structure.

- All instructional decisions—including learning objectives, scenario logic, feedback, and assessments—were finalized through human instructional judgment.  
- Clear constraints in the prompt ensured outputs remained focused, ethical, and workplace-appropriate.  
- This approach balances efficiency with instructional quality, ensuring the final learning experience meets professional and organizational standards.
